Where Will You Go?

Discover Our Beautiful Country

Mozambique offers a variety of experiences for visitors. Explore coastal scenery, learn about local communities, and discover the country's natural heritage.

Bazaruto Archipelago

Located off the coast of Inhambane Province, this island group is known for its sandy beaches, clear waters, coral reefs, and marine life.

Experience: Beaches, snorkeling, and marine scenery.

Maputo

Mozambique's capital offers busy markets, distinctive architecture, coastal views, and a lively food and music scene.

Experience: City life, food, and architecture.

Gorongosa National Park

This protected landscape is home to diverse wildlife and habitats and supports important conservation and community projects.

Experience: Wildlife and nature.

Tofo Beach

Tofo is a coastal destination known for its beach atmosphere, ocean activities, and opportunities to explore marine life.

Experience: Beach activities and ocean scenery.

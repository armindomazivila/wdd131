🇲🇿 Explore Mozambique

Website Planning Document

WDD 131 • W05 PROJECT

Discover the beauty of Mozambique

A digital travel guide designed to help visitors discover places, culture, food, and experiences.

01

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Project Vision

Explore Mozambique will be a simple and engaging website that introduces visitors to interesting destinations, activities, traditional food, and cultural experiences throughout Mozambique.

02

BRAND IDENTITY

Site Name

🇲🇿

Explore Mozambique

I chose the name Explore Mozambique because the website will help visitors discover beautiful places, activities, food, and travel information about Mozambique.

The name is simple, memorable, and clearly communicates the purpose of the website.

03

WHAT THE SITE PROVIDES

Site Purpose

01

Discover Places

Help visitors discover destinations and interesting places throughout Mozambique.

02

Find Activities

Provide information about activities and experiences visitors can enjoy.

03

Experience Culture

Introduce visitors to traditional food, culture, and local experiences.

04

05

VISUAL DESIGN

Color Schema

The color palette is inspired by the colors of the Mozambique flag and is designed to create a strong and recognizable visual identity.

Green

#006B35

Used for headers, navigation, headings, and important site elements.

Yellow

#FCE100

Used for buttons, highlights, borders, and visual accents.

White

#FFFFFF

Used for the main background and content areas.

Dark Gray

#222222

Used for body text to provide good readability.

06

TEXT DESIGN

Typography

HEADINGS

Poppins

Poppins will be used for headings, titles, buttons, and navigation.

BODY TEXT

Roboto

Roboto will be used for paragraphs and general content because it is easy to read.

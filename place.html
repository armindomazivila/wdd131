Mozambique

About Mozambique

Mozambique is a country in southeastern Africa along the Indian Ocean. It is known for its beautiful beaches, wildlife, culture, and natural resources.

The capital city is Maputo. Portuguese is the official language, and the currency is the Mozambican Metical.

Mozambique has a long coastline with many beautiful places to visit, including islands, beaches, and national parks.
